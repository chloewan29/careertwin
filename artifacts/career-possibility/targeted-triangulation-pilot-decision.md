Generated evidenceRequirements or repeated source notes from the same role profile are same-origin duplicates. Mapping and signal counts are not presumed independent because the compiler derives both from the same mapping-rule occurrence.

Audit labels are planning-only: INDEPENDENT_SOURCE, PARTIALLY_INDEPENDENT_SOURCE, SAME_ORIGIN_DUPLICATE, CONTEXT_ONLY and NOT_SEMANTIC_SUPPORT.

## Comparator findings

AUTO is the current union of 10 MODERATE and 8 STRONG capabilities. The compiler score is non-boilerplate expected-evidence count + rich-archetype usage count + twice the mapping-clue count. MODERATE commonly comes from one rich-archetype contribution plus one non-boilerplate evidence contribution, or one direct mapping clue. STRONG combines more rich/evidence contributions and/or mapping clues. These counts explain deterministic state; they do not prove source independence. The current evidence-signal count mirrors mapping-rule occurrences and must not be double-counted as an independent source.
