# Job Copilot Product Principle

## 1) What CareerTwin is

CareerTwin is a human-centric career operating system.

It is not just a resume tool.
It is not just a job search utility.
Job search is one important career moment, not the whole product.

The product should help people understand, organize, and express their professional identity over time.

## 2) What Job Copilot is

Job Copilot is an ambient side-panel companion during real job browsing.

Its role is to help users quickly understand:
- their strengths for this role
- the role's key capabilities
- likely gaps
- hiring risks

The primary user feeling should be:
"I now understand how to tell my story for this role."

## 3) UX principles

- Strengths first.
- Then role capability context.
- Then gaps and risks.
- Analysis should feel honest, useful, and human-centric.
- Avoid generic "AI advice" tone.
- Avoid over-indexing on resume language.
- Support career identity, not just ATS optimization.

## 4) CareerTwin product philosophy

Career memory is the source of truth.

Resume, interview answers, and positioning are projections of deeper career memory.

The system should help users:
- see patterns in their career
- tell a clearer story about themselves

## 5) Phase 1 principle

Phase 1 should support the full job-seeking flow as much as practical:

`job understanding -> matching -> positioning -> interview preparation`

Completeness across this flow is critical for trust and seamlessness.

## Implementation Guardrails

- Start every role analysis with user strengths anchored in career memory evidence.
- Present role capability context before recommendations; avoid generic advice-first output.
- Treat gaps and hiring risks as honest context, not fear-driven messaging.
- Keep resume output as one projection; do not collapse product value into ATS optimization.
- Preserve end-to-end flow coverage in Phase 1: understanding, matching, positioning, interview prep.
