CareerTwin Product Surface Map

CareerTwin 的系统在下面：

Career Memory Engine
Capability Engine
Job Intelligence
Career Copilot

但用户看到的是 5 个 Surface（场景入口）。

Surface 1 — Career Profile

这是 CareerTwin 的主页。

用户第一次进来看到的不是 job list，而是：

Your Career Twin

内容包括：

Career Snapshot

例如：

Analytics / Data Strategy Leader
12+ years experience
Enterprise transformation
Stakeholder leadership
Capability Map

例如：

Program Delivery
Stakeholder Leadership
Analytics Strategy
Transformation
Evidence Highlights

从 EvidencePieces 抽取：

Led enterprise-wide Power BI transformation
Built analytics capability framework
Led APAC measurement programs
Career Direction Signals

例如：

Likely paths:

Senior Analytics Leader
Program Director
Strategy Leader
Surface 2 — Job Hunter

这是你现在正在做的。

入口：

Explore roles

用户看到：

Jobs matched to your career

功能：

Job feed
Match score
Gap analysis
Tailored resume
Application tracker

Pipeline：

JD
↓
extract signals
↓
match EvidencePieces
↓
tailored resume
Surface 3 — Career Transition

用户可以问：

Can I become a Program Manager?

系统会分析：

Target role
↓
Capabilities required
↓
Your EvidencePieces
↓
Gap

输出：

Transferable strengths
Capability gaps
Transition plan
Surface 4 — Promotion Readiness

例如：

Manager → Senior Manager

CareerTwin 会分析：

Promotion signals
Evidence alignment
Missing signals

输出：

Promotion narrative
Evidence support
Leadership signals
Surface 5 — Career Growth

这是最长期的。

用户可以：

Track career growth

系统可以：

Detect capability gaps
Suggest projects
Recommend experiences

例如：

To reach Director level you likely need:

P&L ownership
Organization leadership
Cross-market transformation
